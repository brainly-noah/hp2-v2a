6.Questions

Before you start.

Will it just give them the answers?

No. Worksheets is built so they can't skip to one — it turns the assignment into practice they have to work through. Explain It checks their thinking rather than replacing it.

How is this different from ChatGPT?

A general chatbot answers what's in front of it and forgets. Brainly was built for education and it compounds — the thing they got stuck on in October is what Test Prep drills in November. And there's a person on your account, which no chatbot offers.

What does the coach actually do?

They set your family up in the first week, keep you posted on what your teen has been working on and where they're gaining ground, and answer you directly when you have a question. On the tutor plan, they also handle the match.

What subjects does it cover?

The learning tools cover every subject your teen takes in grades 9 through 12. Tutors are matched to the subject that's giving them trouble — tell us which one and we'll confirm the match before anything is charged.

Can I switch between plans?

Any time. Add a tutor for a hard semester and drop back to the platform afterwards — everything Brainly has learned about your teen carries across.

Is it safe for my teen?

Brainly is built for education rather than adapted from a general-purpose assistant, is age-appropriate by design, and complies with COPPA, GDPR and CPRA.
